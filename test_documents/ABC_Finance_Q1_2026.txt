ABC Finance Company
Corporate Governance Policy
Version 1.0 - Last Updated: January 2026

1. BOARD OF DIRECTORS
The Board of Directors is responsible for overseeing the company's governance framework. The Board has established an Audit Committee.

2. RISK MANAGEMENT
The Risk Management department is responsible for identifying and monitoring financial risks.

3. COMPLIANCE
The company is committed to complying with all applicable regulatory requirements.

4. INTERNAL AUDIT
The Internal Audit department conducts annual audits of the company's operations.

5. POLICY REVIEW
Policies are reviewed as needed.

6. INTERNAL CONTROLS
The company maintains internal controls to ensure accurate financial reporting.

7. DOCUMENTATION
Board meeting minutes are recorded.
