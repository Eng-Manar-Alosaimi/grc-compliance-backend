ABC Finance Company
Corporate Governance Policy
Version 2.0 - Last Updated: April 2026

1. BOARD OF DIRECTORS
The Board of Directors is responsible for overseeing the company's governance framework. The Board has established an Audit Committee, a Risk Committee, and a Remuneration Committee. Each committee operates under a documented charter that defines its roles and responsibilities.

2. RISK MANAGEMENT
The Risk Management department is responsible for identifying, assessing, and monitoring financial risks. The risk management framework is reviewed annually. The Board approves the risk appetite statement on an annual basis.

3. COMPLIANCE
The company is committed to complying with all applicable regulatory requirements. Compliance responsibilities are assigned to the Compliance department.

4. INTERNAL AUDIT
The Internal Audit department conducts annual audits of the company's operations. The department reports functionally to the Audit Committee.

5. POLICY REVIEW
Policies are reviewed annually, or as needed when regulatory changes occur.

6. INTERNAL CONTROLS
The company maintains internal controls to ensure accurate financial reporting. The internal control framework is reviewed periodically.

7. DOCUMENTATION
Board meeting minutes are recorded and retained. Decision logs are maintained by the Board Secretary.

8. WHISTLEBLOWING
The company has a whistleblowing policy that allows employees to report concerns through defined channels.
